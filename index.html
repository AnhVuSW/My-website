Our Planet Is Full of Extraordinary Places

From ancient rainforests to coral kingdoms beneath the sea, Earth holds wonders that have shaped civilisations, inspired artists, and humbled explorers for millennia. This page celebrates seven of the most awe-inspiring natural sites on the planet.

Seven Natural Wonders

  1. Amazon Rainforest — South America Home to roughly 10% of all species on Earth, covering 5.5 million km².
  2. Great Barrier Reef — Australia The world's largest coral reef system, stretching over 2,300 km.
  3. Grand Canyon — United States A mile-deep gorge carved by the Colorado River over 5–6 million years.
  4. Victoria Falls — Zambia / Zimbabwe The world's largest waterfall curtain at 1,708 m wide and 108 m tall.
  5. Mount Everest — Nepal / Tibet Earth's highest peak at 8,849 m above sea level.
  6. Northern Lights — Arctic Circle Plasma streams from the sun collide with Earth's atmosphere, creating vivid curtains of light.
  7. Sahara Desert — North Africa The world's largest hot desert at 9.2 million km², larger than the contiguous USA.
